Privacy Policy

Slop Scorecard Checker

Effective date: September 10, 2026

Overview

Slop Scorecard Checker is a Chrome extension that helps users review scorecard checkbox selections and their corresponding explanations. The extension performs its analysis locally in the user's active browser tab.

Information processed

When the user activates the extension, it processes the information needed to provide the requested review, including:

  • Scorecard phrase and checkbox labels;
  • Checkbox selection states;
  • Text entered in the “Brief explanation of your selections” field; and
  • Comment drafts created or edited in the extension's results panel.

How information is used

This information is used only to compare checkbox selections with their explanations, identify possible inconsistencies or configured writing patterns, display review results, and prepare a user-editable review comment.

Local processing and storage

Scorecard information is processed locally in the active browser tab. The extension does not transmit scorecard content, form data, browsing activity, or comment drafts to the developer or to external servers. Comment drafts may be stored temporarily in browser session storage so they remain available during the current page session.

Sharing and sale of information

The extension does not sell, rent, share, or transfer user information to third parties. It does not use user information for advertising, profiling, or analytics.

Retention and deletion

The developer does not receive or retain user information. Temporary comment drafts are controlled by the browser and normally expire when the applicable tab or page session ends. Users can remove them by closing the applicable tab or clearing the website's session data.

Permissions

The extension uses the activeTab and scripting permissions only after the user activates it. These permissions allow the extension to inspect the current scorecard and display its results panel on that page. The extension does not request persistent access to websites.

Children's privacy

The extension is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the extension's functionality or data practices change. Any update will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
